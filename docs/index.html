        About this framework
        
            
        The Vendor Risk Assessment Framework scores any third-party engagement across six dimensions — co-employment, data sensitivity & privacy, physical safety, site & security access, business continuity and AI/ML model risk — each rated 0–3 for a total of 0–18. The total maps to four risk tiers: Standard (0–5), Enhanced (6–10), High (11–15) and Critical (16–18), and any single dimension scored 3 automatically adds a specialist review and the Enhanced contract clause set.
        
            
        It is built for procurement, legal, security, privacy and GRC teams that need consistent, defensible vendor decisions without a heavyweight platform, and it is aligned with NIST CSF 2.0, ISO/IEC 27001:2022, NIST AI RMF 1.0 and common GDPR, HIPAA, CCPA, SOX and EU AI Act vendor-oversight expectations.
